A 2-minute tour

  1. Customer App: add a Spanish Latte (pick size & milk) and a croissant, choose Delivery, fill in a name, phone and address, then Place order.
  2. Shop Dashboard (PIN 1234): the order rings in New. Press Accept — the customer’s screen switches to “Being prepared”.
  3. Press Dispatch: give it to driver Elie, or choose Delivery company → SwiftRun.
  4. Driver App (Elie, 1111) or Delivery Company (SwiftRun, 5555): accept the job. In the shop press Mark ready, then in the driver or company app press Picked up and Delivered.
  5. Watch the customer’s tracking update at each step, then open Reports in the shop to see sales, payments, driver cash and courier balances.

This demo runs on sample data stored in your browser only. Use “Reset demo” in the orange bar to start over. Real shops get their own secure, cloud-synced setup across all phones.
